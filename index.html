My approach

What I do before I hit “source.”

Good recruitment starts before the first profile appears. I think about the requirement, the candidate pool and the experience the candidate will have throughout the process.

Understand.
Find.
Evaluate.

Requirement→Search→Screen→Coordinate→Onboard
01

Understand the requirement

I want clarity on what is genuinely essential, what is preferred, and what the hiring manager will consider a strong profile.

02

Turn the requirement into a search

I translate the role into search terms, candidate profiles and sourcing channels rather than relying on one generic approach.

03

Screen for the person behind the profile

Experience matters, but so do communication, motivation, expectations, availability and how closely the candidate's actual experience matches the role.

04

Keep the process moving

Recruitment involves follow-ups, interview scheduling, stakeholder coordination and timely communication. Small delays can become candidate drop-offs.

05

Adapt when the requirement changes

If profiles are repeatedly rejected, I would look for the pattern, clarify the feedback and adjust the search instead of simply increasing volume.
